Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 27, 2026
Last updated: August 27, 2026

Privacy at a glance. MWD Extensions develops and distributes software products for ecommerce merchants, developers, and agencies. We do not sell personal information, we do not use customer information for cross-context behavioral advertising, and our software products are not designed to transmit merchant operational data to MWD Extensions during normal use unless expressly stated otherwise.

1. About This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how MWD Extensions ("MWD Extensions," "MWD," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, stores, discloses, and protects information in connection with our websites, software products, downloadable extensions, direct sales, marketplace listings, customer support, and related services.

This Policy applies when you visit our website, purchase or download a product directly from us, obtain a product through a third-party marketplace, communicate with us, request support, or otherwise interact with MWD Extensions.

This Policy does not govern the privacy practices of merchants who use our software in their own businesses. Merchants remain responsible for their own privacy practices and for their compliance with laws applicable to information they process using Magento, Adobe Commerce, RabbitMQ, or other systems.

2. Information We May Collect

Information you provide to us

Depending on how you interact with MWD Extensions, we may receive information such as:

  • Your name.
  • Your email address.
  • Your company or business name.
  • A website, store, Magento, or production domain you provide during checkout, licensing, registration, or support.
  • Purchase, order, invoice, and transaction-related information.
  • Messages, questions, product feedback, and other information you provide when contacting us.
  • Technical information you voluntarily provide when requesting support, such as Magento versions, PHP versions, queue names, configuration summaries, logs, diagnostic reports, error messages, or similar troubleshooting information.

Payment information

Direct payments may be processed by third-party payment providers such as Stripe. Payment providers may collect your payment-card information, billing information, fraud-prevention information, and other transaction data directly.

MWD Extensions does not receive or store your complete payment-card number or card security code. We may receive transaction information from the payment provider, such as your email address, customer name, order amount, payment status, transaction identifiers, and information needed to fulfill and support your purchase.

Information generated during download fulfillment

When a directly purchased product is delivered electronically, we may process temporary information necessary to provide and secure the download, including:

  • Checkout or transaction identifiers.
  • Randomly generated download tokens.
  • Download authorization status.
  • Email-delivery status.
  • Basic request, security, or operational information processed by our infrastructure providers.

Secure product download links may expire after a limited period. The current standard download period for directly purchased products is generally seven days unless otherwise stated at the time of purchase.

3. Information Processed by Our Software Products

MWD Extensions products may process technical or operational data inside a customer's own Magento or Adobe Commerce environment in order to provide their intended functionality.

For example, MWD Queue Manager for Magento 2 may access and process information such as Magento message-queue configuration, RabbitMQ runtime statistics, queue health information, backlog information, consumer information, historical queue metrics, diagnostic information, and related system configuration.

This operational information is processed within systems controlled by the merchant or its hosting provider. MWD Queue Manager is not designed to transmit this operational information to MWD Extensions during normal product use.

RabbitMQ credentials, integration credentials, and other environment-specific secrets configured for the extension are intended to remain within the customer's Magento environment and are not transmitted to MWD Extensions as part of normal product operation.

Queue history and other product-generated operational records may be stored in the customer's Magento database. Alerts generated by the extension may be delivered using the customer's own Magento email configuration.

If a customer voluntarily sends us a diagnostic report, log file, screenshot, configuration information, or other technical material for support purposes, that material becomes information provided to MWD Extensions and will be handled according to this Privacy Policy.

Our support tools may be designed to reduce or redact sensitive information, but customers should review diagnostic information before sending it to us and should not knowingly send passwords, authentication secrets, private keys, payment-card information, or unrelated personal information.

4. Information Collected Automatically

Websites, download infrastructure, email systems, and other internet services necessarily process certain technical information in order to operate securely and reliably. This may include:

  • IP address.
  • Browser and device information.
  • Date and time of a request.
  • Requested URL or resource.
  • Basic server, security, or error-log information.

MWD Extensions does not currently use personal information for targeted advertising or cross-site behavioral advertising.

We do not currently deploy our own advertising cookies or advertising trackers on the MWD Extensions website. Our website and linked third-party services may be hosted or operated by companies that process technical information according to their own privacy policies.
